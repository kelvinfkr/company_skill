# Role pay bands

For each economically important role construct three bands.

1. Market replacement band: external cost to hire/retain a credible replacement.
2. Economic justification band: what the company can rationally pay given value-at-risk, profit-pool leverage, dependency and affordability.
3. Internal-equity band: what adjacent internal roles, executive compensation and equity plans imply.

Do not average the bands mechanically.

## Diagnostics
- overlap exists -> `defensible_zone = overlap`;
- market entirely above economic band -> `structural_talent_gap`;
- internal band below market/economic overlap -> `retention_risk`;
- internal band above both market and economic bands -> `overpayment_or_governance_risk`.

## Economic-justification logic
Use ranges, not fake precision. Consider:
- gross profit or contribution influenced;
- probability/magnitude of value loss if vacant or replaced poorly;
- customer concentration;
- technical or regulatory bottlenecks;
- time-to-replace and internal bench strength;
- company operating-profit and payroll capacity.
