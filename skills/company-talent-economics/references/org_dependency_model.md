# Organization and dependency model

Do not use a universal L1-L8 ladder as the only representation.

## Two-layer role representation

### Native state
- company-native title;
- native level if known;
- BU/function;
- geography;
- IC/manager/executive/sales track.

### Latent role vector
Use ordinal/qualitative fields:
- scope: task, project, team, multi-team, function, company;
- decision authority;
- technical depth;
- people responsibility;
- revenue/profit responsibility;
- customer concentration exposure;
- scarcity/replacement difficulty;
- external market value;
- equity intensity;
- promotion selectivity.

Never claim exact cross-company equivalence such as `Company A Level X = Company B Level Y` without evidence. Say "roughly comparable on scope" and show differences.

## Dependency questions

For each critical role ask:
1. What profit pool or strategic capability does the role affect?
2. What breaks if the role is vacant for 3-6 months?
3. Can relationships/knowledge be transferred to the organization?
4. How many credible substitutes exist internally?
5. How long would external replacement take?
6. Does the role control a concentrated customer/channel/regulatory relationship?
7. Does the role own a technical bottleneck or a generalizable platform?

Classify dependency as low/medium/high/extreme with written evidence.

## Organization reconstruction sources

Prefer:
- official leadership pages;
- annual report organization disclosures;
- appointment/promotion announcements;
- job postings with reporting lines;
- public biographies;
- aggregate public-profile patterns.

A job title is evidence of naming, not of actual organizational scope.
