# Governance, equity incentives, and talent capital allocation

Equity is both compensation and a governance transfer.

## Collect
- total equity incentive pool as % of shares;
- new-issue vs treasury-share source;
- recipient count and broad role groups;
- grant/strike/purchase price;
- vesting/attribution schedule;
- performance conditions;
- accounting share-based-compensation expense;
- executive/director allocations;
- realized/vested shares when disclosed.

## Distinguish values

Do not confuse:
- accounting SBC expense;
- grant-date fair value;
- current mark-to-market value;
- employee net value after purchase/strike price;
- cash actually paid by the employee;
- dilution cost to existing shareholders.

## Useful talent-allocation metrics

When evidence permits:
- equity-incentive recipients / workforce;
- annualized equity value / cash payroll;
- SBC / gross profit;
- incentive dilution / year;
- executive equity / total incentive pool;
- equity compensation value / dividends.

These are signals of how much ownership the company allocates to talent, not proof of individual productivity.
