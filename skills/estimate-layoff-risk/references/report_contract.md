# Report contract

## Required sections

1. Scope, entity, outcome definitions, date, horizons, confidence.
2. 3/6/12-month table by exit mechanism.
3. One-sentence diagnosis and dominant uncertainty.
4. Company pressure regime.
5. Profit-pool map.
6. Dependency graph through role and employment mechanism.
7. Observable leadership allocation regime.
8. Candidate-local signals, labeled and redacted as requested.
9. Favorable, base, and adverse scenarios.
10. Evidence for and against.
11. Indicators that move risk up or down.
12. Limitations, privacy note, and non-legal-advice disclaimer.

Populate `report.json` from `assets/report_spec.json`. Do not use Markdown as the final layout source. Render JSON to DOCX and PDF.

Lead with results. Use intervals and horizons everywhere. Keep public and candidate evidence visually separate. Use readable source titles/URLs. Avoid tables wider than A4. Include page numbers and reference date.

Render every final page to PNG. Inspect clipping, orphan headings, CJK, overlap, whitespace, and citations. Confirm page count and searchable text.
