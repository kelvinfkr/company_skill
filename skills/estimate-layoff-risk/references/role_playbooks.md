# Role and employment playbooks

Read only the relevant sections. Preserve the candidate's native title while mapping it to a comparable role family.

## Product and design

Ask about product lifecycle, revenue/users/orders, funded roadmap, release cadence, assigned engineering/design capacity, metric ownership, overlapping teams, consolidation, and whether work is decision ownership or coordination.

Use product contribution margin, retention/conversion/price effects, launch timing, avoided engineering waste, and option value.

## Sales, business development, and account management

Ask about quota, pipeline coverage, renewals, sold-product margin, territory consolidation, customer concentration, ramp period, named-account ownership, commission changes, and attribution.

Use attributable contribution margin and retention value, not bookings alone.

## Software, data, AI, infrastructure, and security

Ask about production ownership, on-call, critical systems, platform adoption, migration completion, duplicate platforms, outsourcing/cloud substitution, deployed versus demonstration AI, customer commitments, security obligations, and recovery time.

Use protected revenue, reliability loss avoided, unit-cost savings, time-to-market, technical option value, and replacement/recovery cost.

## Research and long-cycle innovation

Ask about funding runway, next milestone, productization, sponsor/board commitment, asset reuse, follow-on investment, and whether the unit is moving under product control.

Use probability-weighted option value less follow-on investment and execution risk. Do not judge only current loss.

## Manufacturing, quality, and supply chain

Ask about orders, backlog, inventory, utilization, shifts/overtime, line transfer, automation, comparative plant cost, SKU lifecycle, supplier volumes, maintenance, quality, safety, and certifications.

Use volume, throughput, yield, avoided downtime/scrap/recall, minimum safe staffing, and plant economics.

## Operations and customer support

Ask about transaction/ticket volume, service levels, customer segment, automation, outsourcing, language/location requirements, escalation ownership, and seasonality.

Use cost per resolved unit, retention/reputation impact, service obligations, and substitution cost.

## Consulting, professional services, agencies, and project work

Ask about billable utilization, bench duration, backlog, contract end, next project, client-requested status, billing rate versus cost, project margin, renewal, and transferable skills.

Use billable contribution, backlog, client continuity, and bench capacity.

## Finance, HR, legal, procurement, communications, and administration

Ask about employees/entities/transactions served, shared services, M&A duplication, ERP/automation, outsourcing, compliance/restructuring workload, span, and routine processing versus scarce judgment.

Use service volume, risk avoided, transaction enablement, and target corporate-cost ratio. Model a possible second wave after restructuring work peaks.

## Healthcare, education, public sector, nonprofit, and regulated service

Ask about tenure/establishment status, appropriation, grant, enrollment/census, service volume, mandated staffing, accreditation, funding end, and internal transfer.

Use funding source, mandate, service demand, and contract/grant continuity.

## Executive and manager roles

Ask about span, layers, decision rights, succession, sponsor status, P&L ownership, integration overlap, delayering, and whether the executive's strategy lost sponsorship.

Use controlled profit pool, decision leverage, layer-removal savings, and transition risk.

## Employment-type branches

### Intern

Ask about end date, conversion headcount, temporary design, project schedule, mentor authority, cohort outcomes, and whether the intern substitutes for an employee. Report early ending and no-conversion separately.

### Probationary employee

Ask about written criteria, review date, documented feedback, role changes since hiring, post-offer hiring freeze, and whether the original requisition still exists. Search current local law before discussing rights.

### Fixed-term employee

Ask about renewal history, funding/contract end, permanent-equivalent roles, notice date, and whether the role survives beyond the term.

### Contractor/vendor/agency worker

Ask about statement-of-work end, vendor consolidation, purchase-order status, conversion rights, client sponsor, and whether savings are immediate at contract end.

### Permanent employee

Ask about redeployment rules, restructuring mechanism, selection pool, location flexibility, and role-family reduction rather than contract expiry.
