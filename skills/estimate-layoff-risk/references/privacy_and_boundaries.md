# Privacy, fairness, and candidate control

## Data minimization

Use a case alias. Do not put names, email, phone, employee IDs, home address, or private profile URLs in filenames or report metadata. Exact compensation is optional; relative role cost or level usually suffices.

## Prohibited inputs

Do not request, infer, or use race, ethnicity, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, pregnancy, family plans, disability, health, genetics, religion, politics, union membership/protected activity, family status, or age except a necessary age-of-majority check. Do not use proxies.

Do not request private coworker performance, pay, health, or discipline.

## Candidate evidence

Treat candidate reports as voluntary and revisable. Allow `private_for_model_only`. Separate observation from interpretation and rumor.

Do not ask for confidential roadmaps, internal financials, customer lists, source code, access logs, performance files, or employee rosters. Non-confidential summaries of observable changes suffice.

## Decision boundary

Estimate risk for the candidate's planning. Do not rank workers for termination or recommend whom an employer should fire.

Allowed next steps include resume updates, lawful achievement documentation, benefits review, savings, networking, internal applications, and qualified legal advice.

Never recommend taking confidential data, evading controls, sabotage, misrepresentation, or prohibited recording.

Keep raw answers local. Redact from the report unless necessary and consented to. State which conclusions depend materially on candidate reports.
