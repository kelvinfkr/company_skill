# Jurisdiction playbooks

Always verify current law and official notice systems when legal consequences matter. These are search aids, not legal conclusions.

## China

Search company announcements, official recruiting accounts, registries, exchange filings, local labor sources, court records when relevant, and credible media.

Terms: `裁员`, `人员优化`, `组织调整`, `降本增效`, `业务收缩`, `末位淘汰`, `竞聘上岗`, `不续签`, `经济性裁员`, `停工停产`, `关闭工厂`.

Distinguish student internship, probation, labor dispatch, service contracting, and employment contracts. Search current law before discussing rights or compensation.

## United States

Search SEC filings, calls, state WARN systems, Department of Labor sources, local authorities, careers pages, and site reporting.

Terms: `WARN notice`, `reduction in force`, `RIF`, `restructuring`, `severance`, `position elimination`, `furlough`, `facility closure`, `redeployment`.

Do not assume WARN covers every event or worker.

## Germany and European Union

Search filings, public works-council/union communications, employment agencies, regulators, and regional reporting.

German terms: `Stellenabbau`, `Massenentlassungsanzeige`, `Betriebsrat`, `Sozialplan`, `Interessenausgleich`, `Standortschließung`, `Kurzarbeit`, `Versetzung`.

Model consultation, selection, notice, redeployment, and timing separately.

## Japan

Search TDnet/EDINET when applicable, company releases, securities reports, local reporting, and careers pages.

Terms: `人員削減`, `希望退職`, `早期退職`, `事業再編`, `構造改革`, `採用凍結`, `配置転換`, `雇い止め`.

Distinguish voluntary retirement, transfer, fixed-term non-renewal, and dismissal.

## United Kingdom

Search Companies House, annual reports, RNS, consultation reporting, local site notices, careers pages, and public union communications.

Terms: `redundancy consultation`, `collective redundancy`, `role at risk`, `restructuring`, `site closure`, `TUPE`, `redeployment`.

## Other jurisdictions

Identify the official securities regulator, corporate registry, labor ministry, collective-redundancy notice system, exchange platform, and local-language terms. If legal sources cannot be verified, report operational risk only.
